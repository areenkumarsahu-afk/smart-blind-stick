
# Project Limitations

The current prototype has the following limitations:

1. No buzzer or vibration motor is included in the documented implementation.
2. Distance readings are displayed through the Serial Monitor.
3. The sensor measures objects within its effective sensing direction.
4. Soft materials may absorb ultrasonic signals.
5. Angled or irregular surfaces may produce unreliable reflections.
6. The sensor has a limited operating range.
7. The prototype does not identify the type of obstacle.
8. The prototype does not provide complete environmental awareness.
9. The system is not a certified mobility assistance device.

These limitations should be considered when evaluating the practical use of the prototype.
