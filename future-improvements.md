
# Future Improvements

The following improvements could be explored in future versions.

## 1. Vibration Feedback

Integrate a vibration motor to provide tactile obstacle notifications.

## 2. Audio Alerts

Add a buzzer to generate audible warnings based on obstacle distance.

## 3. Multiple Ultrasonic Sensors

Use additional sensors to improve obstacle detection coverage.

## 4. Downward Obstacle Detection

Add a downward-facing sensor to investigate the detection of steps and drop-offs.

## 5. Battery Monitoring

Introduce battery-level monitoring and low-battery notifications.

## 6. Improved Physical Design

Develop a compact, lightweight and durable enclosure for the electronics.

## 7. Emergency Location Sharing

Explore GPS and wireless communication for emergency location sharing.

These are proposed improvements and are not implemented in the current version.
